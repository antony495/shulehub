What ShuleHub is

ShuleHub is a mobile app for Android that helps primary schools, secondary schools, colleges, TVETs and universities in Kenya run their daily work. Institutions subscribe to use the software. ShuleHub is software only: we do not sell physical goods, and we do not collect school fees on behalf of institutions.

Homework and marking

Teachers set work, students submit it in the app, and teachers mark it and give feedback.

Results and grading

Teachers publish marks and grades, and students see them in their own account. Each institution chooses its grading scale: CBC, 8-4-4 (KCSE style), TVET or university, and can edit it.

Report cards

A report card for each student with subjects, marks, grades, average and class position, plus teachers’ remarks and the principal’s remark. Students and parents see it in the app, and the school can print the report cards of a whole class at once as a PDF.

Whole-class mark entry

Teachers enter the marks of a whole class on one screen. The grade appears as they type.

Attendance

Teachers take the register for each class, and students and parents see the record.

Announcements and events

Notices go to the whole institution or to one class, and school events appear on the dashboards.

Timetable

Students, teachers and parents see the lessons that apply to them.

Fee records

The institution records what is charged and paid, and confirms M-Pesa payments that students and parents report. A payment can cover more than one term: the extra goes to the next unpaid fees. Administrators also get a fee arrears report.

Library

Teachers and administrators share study materials, and students save the ones they need. Reported items are reviewed by the school.

Parents

A parent sees only their own child’s information, after the institution has confirmed the link.

English and Kiswahili

Switch the app between English and Kiswahili in Settings. There is also a light and a dark display.

Study browser

Students can look up notes and definitions on the web without leaving the app.

How it works for an institution

  1. Register your institution in the app (choose Administrator, then Register your institution).
  2. We review the registration. This is to protect students’ and parents’ information.
  3. Start your free trial. Once approved, you get a 30-day free trial and a school code.
  4. Set up. Use Quick setup to create your classes and subjects in one go (secondary Forms, CBC Grades 1 to 9, TVET/college or university), add teachers, choose your grading scale, and share the school code so students and parents can register.
  5. Subscribe when you are ready to continue after the trial.

Delete your account

You can ask for your ShuleHub account and its data to be deleted at any time.

  1. Students, parents and teachers: open the app, log in, and tap Request account deletion (on your profile for students, or at the bottom of your dashboard for parents and teachers). The request goes to your institution’s administrator, who decides. Records the institution must keep, such as results, report cards and fee records, may be kept by the institution.
  2. Institution administrators: log in, and tap Request account deletion on your dashboard. The request comes to us.
  3. Cannot log in? Email antonythiongo06@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my ShuleHub account”. Include the name on the account, the email you registered with, and your institution’s name. For a child’s account, write as the parent or guardian.

We respond to every request within 14 days of receiving it. If it is approved, we delete the account and the information linked to it within that time, and we tell you what was kept and why.
