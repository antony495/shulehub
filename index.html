What ShuleHub is

ShuleHub is a mobile app for Android that helps primary schools, secondary schools, colleges, TVETs and universities in Kenya run their daily work. Institutions subscribe to use the software. ShuleHub is software only: we do not sell physical goods, and we do not collect school fees on behalf of institutions.

Homework and marking

Teachers set work, students submit it in the app, and teachers mark it and give feedback.

Results

Teachers publish marks and grades, and students see them in their own account.

Attendance

Teachers take the register for each class, and students and parents see the record.

Announcements

Notices go to the whole institution or to one class.

Fee records

The institution records what is charged and paid, and confirms M-Pesa payments that students and parents report.

Parents

A parent sees only their own child’s information, after the institution has confirmed the link.

How it works for an institution

  1. Register your institution in the app (choose Administrator, then Register your institution).
  2. We review the registration. This is to protect students’ and parents’ information.
  3. Start your free trial. Once approved, you get a 30-day free trial and a school code.
  4. Set up. Create your classes, add teachers, and share the school code so students and parents can register.
  5. Subscribe when you are ready to continue after the trial.

Refunds and cancellation

  • Every new institution gets a 30-day free trial, so you can try ShuleHub before paying.
  • You can stop at any time. We do not charge you again unless you choose to renew.
  • Fees already paid are not refunded, except where the law requires it, or where we are unable to provide the service.
  • If you think you were charged by mistake, contact us (see below) and we will look into it.

Delete your account

You can ask for your ShuleHub account and its data to be deleted at any time.

  1. Students, parents and teachers: open the app, log in, and tap Request account deletion (on your profile for students, or at the bottom of your dashboard for parents and teachers). The request goes to your institution’s administrator, who decides. Records the institution must keep, such as results and fee records, may be kept by the institution.
  2. Institution administrators: log in, and tap Request account deletion on your dashboard. The request comes to us.
  3. Cannot log in? Email antonythiongo06@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my ShuleHub account”. Include the name on the account, the email you registered with, and your institution’s name. For a child’s account, write as the parent or guardian.

We respond to every request within 14 days of receiving it. If it is approved, we delete the account and the information linked to it within that time, and we tell you what was kept and why.
