ShuleHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026  |  Version 2.1

ShuleHub is an app that helps schools, colleges, TVETs, universities and other learning institutions run their daily work: homework, results, attendance, announcements and fee records. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why, who can see it, and what choices you have.

1. Who we are and who is responsible

ShuleHub is provided by Antony Thiong’o (“we”, “us”), based in Embu, Kenya.

Each institution that uses ShuleHub decides what information about its students, staff and parents is put into the app, and why. That institution is responsible for that information (in data protection language, the “data controller”). We provide the app and the technical systems that hold the information on the institution’s behalf (a “data processor”). For the accounts of our own users, for approving institutions, and for managing subscriptions, we are also responsible for the information we need to do that.

2. What information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. The information depends on who you are in the app.

WhoInformation
Everyone Full name, email address, phone number, the role you use (student, teacher, administrator or parent), the institution you belong to, and a unique account ID. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. We cannot read it. When you create a student or parent account, we also record the date and the version of this policy that you accepted.
Students Admission number, the school code you used to join, class (programme, level and term), homework answers, marks, grades and teacher feedback, published results, attendance records, fee records (amounts charged, paid and due), and fee payments you submit (M-Pesa confirmation code, amount and the phone number used). Until your institution’s administrator approves your registration, we hold your registration request: your name, email, phone number, admission number, institution, and the date you accepted this policy. Administrators The decisions you record, such as approving a student, confirming a payment or a parent, or deciding a deletion request, with your account ID and the time.
Institutions that register The institution’s name, type and country; the name, email address and phone number of the person who registers it; the date they accepted this policy; our decision on the registration; and, once approved, the school code, subscription plan and expiry date, and notes about subscription payments.
Anyone who asks for their account to be deleted Your name, email address, role, institution and the date of the request (and your admission number if you are a student), and the decision on it: approved, not approved with the reason, or completed.

Anyone who uses the library The list of library items you save for later (we store only which items, and when), and any report you send about an item: the item, the reason you chose, the note you typed (up to 500 characters) and the date.

Everyone (technical) If the app crashes, a crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics. The app also remembers the role you last used on your phone, so it can open the right screen. To send you notifications, the app saves a token that identifies your phone, together with your account ID, role, institution and the time. What we do not collect: your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or advertising ID. The app has no advertising, and we do not sell personal information. Our website does not use cookies or analytics.

School fees are paid outside the app, using M-Pesa. ShuleHub does not handle school fees. It only records the confirmation code and amount that a student or parent types in, so the institution can confirm the payment. M-Pesa is run by Safaricom, and its own privacy terms apply to that payment.

An institution’s subscription to ShuleHub is paid separately: directly to us, or by card or M-Pesa through Paystack once online payment is available. Paystack Payments Kenya Limited is a payment service provider licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya, and its own privacy terms apply to those payments. Its systems may process and store payment data outside Kenya.

3. Children and parents

Many students are under 18. In Kenya, the law requires the consent of a parent or guardian before personal information about a child is processed, and the processing must protect the child’s rights and best interests. The institution is responsible for obtaining that consent. Students under 18 should use ShuleHub only with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian.

When a student or a parent creates an account, the app asks them to confirm that they have read this policy. The app records the date and the version of the policy that was accepted. This confirmation does not replace the consent that the institution must obtain from the parent or guardian of a student under 18.

Each institution is responsible for obtaining the consent of a parent or guardian for its students under 18, for example when the parent or guardian signs the admission form. ShuleHub does not collect this consent for the institution.

A parent can ask to be linked to a child. The institution’s administrator checks the request and confirms it. Only after that can the parent see the child’s homework, results, attendance, announcements and fee records, and pay the child’s fees. The administrator can remove the link at any time.

4. How we use information

  • To create and protect your account and to know which screens and information you may see.
  • To run the institution’s work: homework and marking, results, attendance, announcements, fee records and payment confirmation.
  • To review and approve institutions that register, and to manage each institution’s subscription.
  • To handle requests to delete accounts.
  • To find and fix problems, using crash reports.
  • To follow the law and to protect users and the service.

We use information for these purposes only. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Who can see information

  • Students see their own information and their own class’s homework, announcements and attendance register.
  • Teachers see the students of their own institution that they need to teach, mark and record attendance for.
  • Administrators see the information of their own institution.
  • Parents see only the child the institution has confirmed for them, and only that child’s attendance.
  • Your saved library list is visible only to you. A library report is seen by the administrator of the institution that owns the item, or by us for items we added.
  • The owner of ShuleHub approves institutions, manages subscriptions and administrators’ deletion requests, and can technically reach the database when needed to give support or fix problems.

Information from one institution is not shown to another. We use Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics and, for payments, Cloud Functions) to run the app. Google processes information for us under its own data protection terms. Paystack processes subscription payments, as described above. We do not share personal information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

6. Where information is stored

Information is stored on Google’s servers in Johannesburg, South Africa, which is outside Kenya. Payment data handled by Paystack may be processed in other countries too. Where information leaves Kenya, we rely on the protections required by the Data Protection Act, 2019.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information while the account is active and for as long as the institution needs it for its school records. When an account is deleted, we delete or anonymise the related information within 14 days of the request. We keep records that the law requires us to keep, such as financial records, for as long as Kenyan tax and accounting law requires. Registration requests that were not approved are kept only so that the person can see the reason, and we delete them after 90 days. Crash reports are kept for a limited time set by Google.

8. Security

  • Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, and information is sent over encrypted connections.
  • Access rules on the server make sure each person sees only what their role allows.
  • New institutions are checked and approved by us before they can use the app, and students and parents need the institution’s approval before they see anything.
  • Access stops for an institution when its subscription ends.

No system is completely secure. Please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

If we become aware of a breach that affects personal information, we will tell the affected institution without unnecessary delay and, where the law requires, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • know what information is held about you and why;
  • see a copy of it;
  • have wrong information corrected;
  • object to some uses of it;
  • ask for it to be deleted.

For a child, a parent or guardian can use these rights. Contact the institution or contact us (section 12). If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

10. Deleting your account and data

From the app. Log in and tap Request account deletion:

  • Students: on My Profile.
  • Parents and teachers: at the bottom of your dashboard.
  • Institution administrators: at the bottom of your dashboard.

For students, parents and teachers, the request goes to your institution’s administrator, because some records belong to the institution and may have to be kept. For administrators, the request comes to us. We respond to every request within 14 days of receiving it. You can see the decision in the app. If it is approved, we delete the account and the information linked to it within 14 days of your request. If it is not approved, the app shows the reason, and you can still contact us by email or complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

A parent’s request deletes the parent’s own account and details. It does not delete the child’s records, which belong to the institution.

When an account is deleted, your saved library list and the library reports you sent are deleted with it.

By email, if you cannot log in:

  1. Send an email to antonythiongo06@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my ShuleHub account”.
  2. Write the name on the account, the email address used to register, and your institution’s name. For a child’s account, write as the parent or guardian.
  3. We will confirm your identity and, if the institution agrees, delete the account and the information linked to it within 14 days of your request.

Some records belong to the institution, such as fee records and results, and may have to be kept by the institution for legal or school record reasons. We will tell you what is kept and why.
